## Brand & Style

This design system embodies serene clarity, spatial precision, and high-end architectural contemplation. Tailored for architects, interior designers, urban planners, and real estate developers orchestrating high-fidelity AI render passes, the visual language mirrors a quiet Nordic harbor veiled in maritime mist during the low golden hour.

The aesthetic fuses **refined Glassmorphism** with **tactile architectural instrumentation**. It avoids neon digital tropes in favor of deep oceanic abyssal layers, crystalline frost overlays, ambient refractions, and surgical hairline delineations. Controls feel like milled aluminum and optical studio instruments submerged behind polished ice, giving creators a calm, high-focus canvas for generative physical spaces.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in abyssal navy tones illuminated by sub-zero spectral highlights and punctuated by a singular warm low-angle sun ray:

- **Abyssal Base (`#08131F`):** The master canvas, representing cold arctic deep water. It grounds the UI with absolute contrast.
- **Glass Panel Surface (`#16314A`):** Applied primarily as translucent glaze (`rgba(22, 49, 74, 0.40)`) or solid fallback at 92% opacity.
- **Structural Slate (`#2E4F6B`):** Structural divisions, non-active tracks, and secondary container fills.
- **Muted Steel (`#5F7F98`):** Low-emphasis text, grid reference lines, and inactive state icons.
- **Frosted Hairline (`#A9BDC9`):** Sub-headings, secondary values, and perimeter rims (`rgba(169, 189, 201, 0.28)`).
- **Crisp Ice White (`#E8EFF3`):** High-priority titles, critical readouts, and active glyphs.
- **Ice Accent (`#CFE3F0`):** The primary kinetic signal; used for primary button fills, gradient overlays, and active sliders.
- **Sun Glint Warm Accent (`#E8C98A`):** The golden hour glint. Used with surgical restraint for studio credits, compute tokens, sun/azimuth spatial adjustments, and warning highlights.

All text combinations against glass surfaces maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1.

## Typography

Typography establishes an architectural cadastre: clean, unhurried, and technically balanced.

- **Headings & Display Roles:** Rendered in **Space Grotesk** set to Light (`300`) with uppercase styling and wide tracking (`0.15em` to `0.2em`). This evokes technical schematics and luxury gallery signage.
- **Body, UI Controls, & Numerical Data:** Rendered in **Manrope** across weights `400` (Regular), `500` (Medium), and `600` (Semi-bold). Manrope provides geometric stability and legible small-scale rendering inside dense control docks, render prompts, and optical parameter badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The studio operates on a **contextual fixed-dock & fluid-viewport grid**:

- **Viewport Canvas:** The center viewport hosts 3D space and generated imagery, fluidly filling the residual area.
- **Tool Shelves & Inspectors:** Docked glass sidebars enforce a standard structural width of `360px` on desktop (`1440px+`) and collapse into slide-over sheets or bottom drawer capsules on tablet and mobile.
- **Rhythm & Grid:** A base 8px increment governs padding and gaps. Canvas margins expand to `2.5rem` (`40px`) on ultra-wide architectural monitors, allowing preview artboards breathing room away from system docks.
- **Reflow:** On screens `< 768px`, dense parameter grids reflow from 3-column dial clusters into single-column expandable accordion rows with pill controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is attained through refractive physics, deep water falloff, and crystalline rim highlights rather than crude opaque drop shadows:

1. **Base Ocean Layer (Z0):** Flat background color `#08131F`.
2. **Glass Shelf / Panel Layer (Z1):**
   - Background: `rgba(22, 49, 74, 0.40)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(26px) saturate(150%)`
   - Perimeter Hairline: `1px solid rgba(169, 189, 201, 0.28)`
   - Light Catch Highlight: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(232, 239, 243, 0.20)`
   - Ambient Shadow: `0 20px 50px rgba(4, 10, 18, 0.60)`
3. **Floating Overlays & AI Prompt Bars (Z2):**
   - Background: `rgba(22, 49, 74, 0.65)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(32px) saturate(180%)`
   - Perimeter Hairline: `1px solid rgba(207, 227, 240, 0.45)`
   - Light Catch Highlight: `inset 0 1.5px 1.5px 0 rgba(232, 239, 243, 0.35)`
   - Ambient Shadow: `0 30px 70px rgba(4, 10, 18, 0.85)`
4. **Reduced Transparency Fallback:** When `prefers-reduced-transparency` or low-power GPU mode is active, surfaces step down to solid `#16314A` at `92%` opacity with blur disabled.

## Shapes

The design system uses a deliberate juxtaposition between wide, curved architectural glass enclosures and tight pill-shaped mechanical switches:

- **Panels, Canvases, & Structural Cards:** Enforce an extra-large `28px` corner radius (`rounded-xl` extended), mimicking thick polished glass tiles with melted, liquid-smooth borders.
- **Interactive Controls, Pills, & Inputs:** Enforce continuous pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for sliders, command pills, chips, and tactile toggles.
- **Dial Rings & Visual Gauges:** Complete `100%` circles with crisp micro-notches.

## Components

### 1. Primary Buttons
- **Geometry:** Pill silhouette (`border-radius: 9999px`), `h-11`, padding `0 1.75rem`.
- **Surface:** Subtle gradient from `#CFE3F0` to `#A9BDC9`.
- **Text:** `#08131F`, font weight `600`, tracking `0.04em`.
- **States:** Hover triggers `brightness(1.08)` and an outer bloom `0 0 20px rgba(207, 227, 240, 0.35)`. Active scale drops to `0.98`.

### 2. Secondary & Glass Controls
- **Geometry:** Pill silhouette, translucent glass base (`rgba(22, 49, 74, 0.40)`).
- **Rim:** `1px solid rgba(169, 189, 201, 0.28)`.
- **Text:** `#E8EFF3`, hover border shifts to `#CFE3F0`.

### 3. Sentence AI Command Bar
- **Surface:** Floating Z2 glass capsule centered at the bottom of the viewport.
- **Input Text:** `#E8EFF3` with placeholder in `#5F7F98`.
- **Accessories:** Integrated compute token badge featuring `#E8C98A` (Sun Glint Warm Accent) and an embedded pill trigger to dispatch render jobs.

### 4. Sliders (Lighting, Lens, Atmospheric Density)
- **Track:** `4px` height, filled with `#2E4F6B`. Active progression filled with `#CFE3F0`.
- **Thumb:** `18px` solid circular disc in Crisp Ice White (`#E8EFF3`) with shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Hover:** Thumb expands to `20px` with a subtle halo of `rgba(207, 227, 240, 0.3)`.

### 5. Circular Dials & Capsule Gauges
- **Sun Azimuth & Pitch Dial:** 64px circular glass module with a rotating sun ray pointer tipped in `#E8C98A`.
- **Aspect Ratio Segmented Pills:** Grouped glass container housing discrete pill segments; active segment shifts to `#2E4F6B` background with `#E8EFF3` typography.

### 6. Chips & Attribute Tags
- **Surface:** `h-7`, pill-shaped, `rgba(46, 79, 107, 0.35)` background with `1px` border in `rgba(169, 189, 201, 0.18)`.
- **Text:** Label-sm in `#A9BDC9`. Active or selected chips illuminate with an Ice Accent rim (`#CFE3F0`).

### 7. Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Radio Buttons:** Outer circular ring in `rgba(169, 189, 201, 0.4)`, active state reveals a centered `8px` glowing disc in `#CFE3F0`.
- **Checkboxes:** `18px` rounded box (`border-radius: 6px`) in `#16314A` with `#2E4F6B` border; checked state fills with `#CFE3F0` and renders checkmark glyph in `#08131F`.
